Future Body

I personally believe that absolute gender equality would be possibly achieved only when women no longer have a reproductive function and are no longer in reproductive roles. Only at that time, women can truly be regarded as independent individuals and can freely control their bodies.

 

In my vision of the future, women can change any parts of their body, especially their sexual characteristics. For example, they can choose to remove their uterus because they don't need to give birth. They can have their breasts removed or change the size of their breasts at will because they do not need to breastfeed. They can be any kind of human being they want to be.

 

So in this project, I am trying to show the possibility for women to change their body parts.
