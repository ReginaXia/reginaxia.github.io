Cyber Angel (2024)

Individual Animation: 

- character design and modeling

- environmental design and modeling

- animation​​

​

​​Introduction:

This is my first attempt at creating an animated short using Maya, telling the story of an angel quietly sleeping in cyberspace. As the moonlight filters in, she gradually awakens, as if wandering between technology and dreams. This work aims to convey a sense of finding hope in the darkness, exploring the magical intersection of light and dreams in the digital world.
